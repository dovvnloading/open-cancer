## Scientific question, literature and permission boundary

It does not ask whether SINEs alone are biologically autonomous or whether either annotation measures current somatic retrotransposition.

## Panel, endpoint and BEFORE-SCORING audit

The immutable eligible cohort is **all 55 source rows / 11 source `Order` categories** (source `species` identifier), sorted by species identifier, no synonym join, sample selection or outcome-based exclusions. Reject duplicate identifier/order conflict or missing, nonfinite, negative count or predictor; `Necropsies` must be a positive integer; `L1_Counts` and `SINEs_Counts` must be nonnegative integers; `maximum_longevity_y` and `Contig_N50` must be positive and `Assembly_BUSCO_Scores` within [0,100]. Incomplete/invalid or fewer than 8 eligible orders is a **data gate -> untestable** (do not quietly drop observations). Keep **all** L1-zero species via `log1p(0)=0`. The panel is already outcome-selected upstream: zero-recorded-neoplasia *or* zero-recorded-malignancy species were excluded by the authors; the test cannot estimate general mammalian risk.

Primary: the source decimal `NeoplasiaPrevalence` with denominator `Necropsies`. **Before fitting**, inspect each literal decimal token using base-ten `Decimal` arithmetic. For tokens strictly between 0 and 1, set the compatible rate interval [c-0.5*10^e-2e-15, c+0.5*10^e+2e-15], clipped to [0,1], where e is the decimal exponent of the token; k is compatible iff integer 0<=k<=n and k/n falls inside this interval inclusive (exact rational comparison). For literal endpoint 0 or 1, require exact k=0 or k=n. Compute **all** compatible integers, without assuming precision or uniqueness. The nominal k is nearest integer to n*c with decimal ROUND_HALF_EVEN; it must lie in the compatible set, which must be nonempty. Record n, nominal k, compatible sets, display precision, maximum residual and total n/k. If there are >4096 joint candidate assignments, mark primary **untestable** rather than approximate unenumerated ambiguity. If 2–4096, refit all primary candidate assignments with all their jackknife scores, give the full delta/interval envelope and require the same directional verdict for every assignment; otherwise call the directional claim inconclusive. Do not treat a rounded fraction as an independently observed numerator.

Secondary **only if a separate gate passes**: repeat the exact count audit on `MalignancyPrevalence`; require every nominal malignancy k to be no larger than the corresponding neoplasia k, uniquely identified numerators for both, and check `Ratio` against k_m/k_n at its own displayed decimal precision (including k_n>0). The [Compton data dictionary conflict](../mhc-pathology-prevalence/) means `MalignancyKnown` cannot be silently used as the denominator-linked malignant count: PR #86 found `Malignant/RecordsWithDenominators` compatible instead. This is an internal *arithmetic* reconciliation for S1, not proof of shared individual-record provenance. If any check fails, **withhold secondary analysis**, record every failure, and never let it affect the primary result. Malignancy prevalence is neither the probability of progression given a neoplasm nor a prospective rate.

## Exactly one model comparison

Use two **binomial ridge logistic** regressions on exactly the same source species, k/n endpoints, folds, and penalty. The baseline continuous columns (in this order) are `log1p(L1_Counts)`, **maximum longevity in years** `maximum_longevity_y` (unlogged), `Assembly_BUSCO_Scores` (unlogged), and `log1p(Contig_N50)` (N50 in bases). Extended model appends **only** `log1p(SINEs_Counts)`. Natural logs, no further annotations, no composite L1+SINE count, interactions, nonlinear feature search, imputation, taxonomy dummies, fold tuning or outcome-driven filtering. N50's fixed log transform is scale stabilization rather than another candidate feature.

For *every* training fold, standardize each continuous column by its unweighted **training species** mean and population SD (SD zero -> 1). Freeze these parameters before the held-out order is predicted. Intercept is unpenalized; penalize all slopes equally with predeclared **lambda=1**. Fit by damped Newton with positive-definite Cholesky Hessian and stable sigmoid/softplus, starting intercept from half-event smoothed training pooled prevalence, zero slopes. Minimize `sum_i[n_i*softplus(eta_i)-k_i*eta_i] + 0.5*lambda*sum(slopes²)`. Permit <=100 accepted Newton steps, <=45 step-halving candidates/step, Armijo coefficient 1e-4 and objective floating allowance 1e-12*max(1,abs(objective)). Require max absolute penalized gradient <=max(1e-8,1e-10*sum training n_effective); failures must be surfaced, never discarded. Retain optimization diagnostics and test bounds, **not exact iteration counts/floats**.

For every order: fit both models afresh on **all other orders**, with training-only standardization, and predict every held-out species. Species binomial log loss `-[k*log(p)+(n-k)*log1p(-p)]/n`; clip p for **scoring only** to [1e-12,1-1e-12], count clips. Mean species loss within each held-out order, then mean these order losses **equally**, independent of sample size/order size. Primary gain **delta = baseline loss - baseline+SINE loss**; positive is improvement. Also report necropsy-weighted loss descriptively, all 11 order gains, fraction improving, individual predictions and training-fold diagnostics. No random species split.

## Uncertainty, multiplicity and prespecified falsification

For each order g: **delete it from the dataset and rerun the full leave-one-order-out fitting/scaling/scoring procedure** on all remaining orders, for both models. No deletion of previously computed scores in place of refitting. With G=11, `q_g = G*delta - (G-1)*delta_(-g)`; bias-corrected center = mean q; `SE = sqrt(sum((q_g-center)^2)/(G*(G-1)))`. The approximate **95% two-sided** interval is center +/- `2.2281388519649385*SE` (t_10,0.975). Keep the unadjusted delta separate from this center, and report every full-refit deleted-order result and pseudovalue. It is an approximate *order-level* jackknife on selected, uneven, related clades, not a calibrated independent-animal confidence interval.

The primary endpoint is predeclared and alone decides the verdict. If the secondary gate passes, compute the same full order-jackknife secondary scores but **do not present a second unadjusted confirmatory 95% claim**: additionally show simultaneous conservative **Bonferroni two-endpoint 97.5% two-sided intervals** using `t_10,0.9875 = 2.633766915711`, for *both* endpoints. Secondary inference is exploratory; a malignancy-only apparent gain does not rescue a failed primary claim. If the secondary gate fails, multiplicity is one prespecified primary endpoint, secondary untested.

In addition to **all 11** deleted-order refits, run the following separately and report adverse cases without selection:

1. **Equal-species training weights:** effective n for every training species = that fold's mean original n; preserve its k/n, total training weight and raw held-out test n; lambda 1.
2. **Penalty robustness:** rerun separately lambda=0.1 and lambda=10, never select on scores.
3. **Assembly-quality restricted panel:** require BUSCO >=90 and Contig_N50 >=100,000 bases (thresholds fixed before scores); rerun full held-out-order score using exactly the eligible subset. If fewer than 8 orders, mark this sensitivity *untestable*, which blocks a positive consistent verdict; never change threshold or impute.
4. **N50 quality-transform stress:** within each training fold replace log1p(N50) by its winsorized value at the **training fold's** 10th and 90th percentiles (linear interpolation of sorted training values) and clip held-out values using **those training cutoffs**, then standardize training-only as usual. This retains all primary species/11 orders. No outcome information in cutoff determination.

Record a source-observation caveat: source's zero-neoplasia / zero-malignancy exclusions cannot be repaired with available S1; do **not** manufacture zero rows or call a subset-deletion sensitivity a population estimate. Other nLTR metrics are out of scope *even after adverse scoring*.

## Frozen verdict labels (exhaustive)

- **Untestable**: source or licence gate, 55-row/11-order prefit denominator/rounding gate, more than 4096 ambiguity combinations, fewer than 8 eligible orders, or any required primary/complete-refit calculation invalid. An invalid assembly restriction is reported as *sensitivity untestable* and prevents positive consistency; if otherwise evaluable it leaves primary verdict inconclusive.
- **Consistent** (limited SINE incremental transport under this rule): **all** compatible numerator assignments have delta>0 and jackknife lower>0; >half the held-out orders improve; every deleted-order delta>0; all four named robustness gains are >0 with successful fits, including the quality restriction with >=8 orders. These are stringent conjunctions; consistency is not proof of a biological effect.
- **Falsified** (non-improvement of this frozen predictive claim only): **all** compatible assignments have delta<=0 and jackknife **upper<0**. Report the upper bound explicitly. It does not imply biological absence of a SINE contribution.
- **Inconclusive**: every other evaluable pattern, including a zero-crossing uncertainty interval, adverse sign/order-deletion/assembly sensitivity without a negative interval, or incompatible numerator-assignment verdicts.
